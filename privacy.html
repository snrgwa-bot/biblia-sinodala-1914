Privacy Policy — Biblia Ortodoxă 1914

Effective date / Data intrării în vigoare: July 13, 2026

Rezumat (RO): Textul Bibliei și notițele tale rămân pe dispozitivul tău. Nu cerem cont, nu afișăm reclame și nu folosim servicii de analiză. Când folosești funcția de explicații AI, întrebarea ta este trimisă, prin serverul nostru, către serviciul Google Gemini pentru a genera răspunsul. Nu vindem datele tale.

Summary (EN): The Bible text and your notes stay on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics. When you use the AI explanation feature, your question is sent through our server to Google's Gemini service to generate the answer. We do not sell your data.

This policy describes how Essential Dev Team ("we," "us") handles information when you use Biblia Ortodoxă 1914 (the "App"). We build our apps to collect the minimum data needed to make them work.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The complete Bible text is bundled inside the App and read offline. Your bookmarks, highlights, reading position, and settings are stored locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud device backup if you enable backups). We cannot access this data. The App does not require an account.

2. AI Explanations (Google Gemini)

The App includes an optional AI feature that explains verses and defines words. When you use it:

  • The text of your question or the selected verse/word is sent to our secure proxy server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI service to generate the answer.
  • We do not attach your name, email, or any account identifier to these requests — the App has no accounts.
  • We do not store your questions on our server; they are processed transiently to produce the answer.
  • Google processes the request under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Please do not include personal information (names, addresses, etc.) in AI questions.

If you configure your own Gemini API key in Settings, your questions are sent directly from your device to Google using your key, and our server is not involved.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We receive anonymized transaction records (for example, that a subscription is active) but never your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No user accounts and no collection of names, emails, or phone numbers
  • No location tracking (the biblical map uses fixed historical coordinates and never requests your location)
  • We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the California CCPA/CPRA

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All personal data lives on your device: delete the App to remove it. AI questions are not retained by us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK, California, and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be satisfied on-device; for anything else, email us and we will help within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.
